## Decision rule

Rank a change only after all safety gates for its scenario pass. Then prefer the largest repeatable improvement in the plan's primary bottleneck, using median for typical behavior and p95/p99 for user-visible tail behavior. Treat changes inside overlapping bootstrap confidence intervals as inconclusive until more repetitions are collected.

For metrics where lower is better (latency, bytes, memory, counts), improvement is `1 - candidate / baseline`. For throughput and success/detection rates, improvement is `candidate / baseline - 1`. Raw observations remain authoritative when distributions are multimodal or contain outliers.
